Hours

        
Pick a wheel, then click or drag across the hours it should play. Hours are UTC, with your local time underneath.

        

        

      

      

        

          
Content

          
          
          
        

        
Each folder in content/ is a type, and its subfolders are tags a slot can pick. Tracks in a rare folder are picked much less often. Add audio files, then run npm run pipeline so they can be scheduled.

        

      

      

        
Wheels

        
Each wheel plays its slots in order, then repeats. A slot picks a random track of its type. Highlighted tags narrow the pick to tracks with all of those tags.

        
Each clock is an estimate of one hour: every slot gets the average length of its tracks, and the wheel repeats (lighter slices) until the hour is full. On air, a wheel carries on from where it left off rather than restarting on the hour.
